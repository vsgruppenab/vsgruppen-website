# Prompt till JsonCut AI Director — "Det läcker" (20 s showreel)

Ladda upp `assets/vs-logo-vit.png` i projektet innan du klistrar in. Byt `vs-logo-vit.png`
nedan om filen får ett annat namn i JsonCut.

---

Build a 20-second motion-graphics reel for a Swedish plumbing company, VS Gruppen AB.
Style: flat 2D motion graphics, clean vector, agency-grade — think product-launch explainer,
not slideshow. No stock footage, no photos, no 3D. Nothing on screen may ever be fully static
until the end card.

## Deliverables
- Primary: 1920×1080, 30 fps, exactly 20.0 s.
- Second variant from the same project: 1080×1920 (9:16). Keep all content inside a centered
  safe area so nothing is cut or crowds the edges; text sizes must stay identical between formats
  (size everything relative to the shorter side).
- No audio. A Swedish voiceover will be recorded and added later. The film must work completely
  without sound: every message is on screen as text.

## Brand (use exactly)
- Cobalt blue `#1535cb` (primary), navy `#0a1650` (headlines, end-card background),
  alert red `#e8452f` (urgency, phone number, step 1 marker), light background `#f0f3fe`,
  soft ink `#4d5a75` (secondary text), pale blue `#becdf8` (URL on navy).
- Font: Poppins. Headlines weight 800, secondary text 600.
- Logo: uploaded asset `vs-logo-vit.png` (white logotype, use only on the navy end card).
- Phone number, always written with these exact spaces: `076 882 70 70`
- Do not invent extra text. Only the Swedish strings quoted below may appear on screen.

## Motion language (apply throughout)
- Scene changes are panel wipes: the new scene slides in from the right as a full-frame panel
  with a thin vertical accent bar (≈1.4 % of the shorter side) on its leading edge; the old scene
  slides left by about 30 % and dims. Duration 0.5 s, cubic ease-out. Scenes overlap by 0.5 s so
  there is never an empty frame.
- Headline text enters word by word, staggered ≈0.1 s per word, with a spring overshoot
  (drops in from ~90 % of its own height below, overshoots slightly, settles). Secondary text
  enters the same way but gentler.
- Graphics land with a fast cubic ease-out (≈0.8 s), no overshoot, unless a beat says otherwise.
- Background: on the light panels, 4 short, very faint (6 % opacity) cobalt rounded bars at
  different heights drift slowly sideways at different speeds the whole time — constant motion,
  never distracting.
- Line art: navy strokes, ≈2.6 px at 1080p, round caps and joins, no fills except where stated.

## Scenes and timing (seconds)

### S1 · Cold open — 0.0 to 3.7  (accent bar: red)
- A single large cobalt drop falls from above centre and hits an invisible floor at 0.47 s,
  accelerating (ease-in). On impact: three thin cobalt ellipse rings explode outward and fade
  (0.2 s apart), four small droplets arc up and out, and a flat cobalt puddle (20 % opacity)
  spreads beneath.
- At 0.6 s the headline slams in, word by word: **`DET LÄCKER.`** — very large (≈13 % of the
  shorter side), navy, weight 800.
- At 1.5 s below it, smaller (≈4.6 %), soft ink, weight 600: **`Hemma. Just nu.`**

### S2 · The toilet — 3.2 to 7.5  (accent bar: cobalt)
- Side-view toilet in navy line art assembles itself: the floor line draws left→right (0.6 s);
  the bowl and seat rise up from below; the cistern drops in from above and lands on the back of
  the seat; a vertical supply pipe with a coupling nut slides in from the left and connects into
  the bottom of the cistern. All land by ≈1.0 s with cubic ease-out.
- From 0.9 s the coupling nut drips: a drop every 0.47 s, each falling ≈0.4 s (ease-in), small
  ripple ring on the floor. A cobalt puddle (22 % opacity) under the pipe grows one step per drop,
  spreading in under the toilet.
- The whole illustration slowly pushes in from 100 % to 109 % scale across the scene.
- At 1.1 s, below the illustration, headline word by word (≈6.4 %): **`Vatten på golvet.`**

### S3 · Shut off — 7.1 to 11.4  (accent bar: red)
- A horizontal pipe (navy outline, rounded ends) shoots in from the left to centre (0.8 s).
  Inside it cobalt water flows fast left→right (light streak dashes moving along it).
- At 0.4 s a shut-off valve drops in from above onto the pipe's midpoint: navy body, red lever
  standing straight up with a round knob.
- At 1.5 s the lever snaps 90° to horizontal with a hard spring overshoot (~10° past, then back).
  On the snap the whole frame punches to 103.5 % scale and settles over 0.3 s. Flow stops dead;
  the water downstream of the valve drains to empty over 0.5 s.
- Right after the snap (1.65 s): a red circle badge with a white **`1`** springs in, and next to
  it the headline word by word (≈5 %): **`Stäng av huvudkranen`**

### S4 · Call — 10.9 to 15.8  (accent bar: red)
- A large alert-red disc (≈16 % of the shorter side) with a white phone-handset glyph springs in
  with overshoot. Ring pulses: thin red circles expanding from the disc to 175 % and fading,
  every 0.5 s, three in flight at once.
- At 0.3 s a navy circle badge **`2`** springs in with the headline (≈5 %): **`Ring oss direkt`**
- At 0.7 s the phone number pops in one character at a time (≈0.07 s apart), each character
  springing from 60 % scale: **`076 882 70 70`** — huge (≈11.5 %), alert red, weight 800.
- At 1.8 s a small navy pill badge **`24/7`** (white text) and next to it, soft ink, ≈3.6 %:
  **`Jour i Linköping, året om`**

### S5 · End card — 15.3 to 20.0  (accent bar: red, panel background navy `#0a1650`)
- This panel does NOT slide out. It must be perfectly still for the last 2 seconds so the final
  frame works as a thumbnail.
- 0.2 s: logo `vs-logo-vit.png` springs in (70 % → 100 % scale, fades in), width ≈42 % of the
  shorter side, centred.
- 0.7 s: a thin red line (≈0.4 % tall) draws left→right under the logo to the logo's width.
- 0.9 s, white, weight 600, ≈4 %: **`Rörmokare i Linköping · Dygnet runt`**
- 1.3 s, white, weight 800, ≈7.4 %: **`076 882 70 70`**
- 1.9 s, pale blue `#becdf8`, weight 400, ≈3.4 %: **`vsgruppen.se`**
- Everything is settled by 2.7 s. Hold to 20.0 s.

## Hard rules
- Do not mention forms, quotes, home visits, prices, ROT, or a five-step process anywhere.
  This film is only: it leaks → shut the main valve → call the emergency line.
- Do not translate, rephrase or "improve" the Swedish strings. Keep Swedish characters (å ä ö).
- Total length exactly 20.0 s. No fade to black.
- Mark the phone number, the city name `Linköping`, the URL, the logo and the four brand colours
  as project variables so they can be swapped later without re-editing.

When the build is done, review it against this brief and report anything you could not do
exactly as specified — do not silently substitute.
